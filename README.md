# ChemStereo Lab — Free edition

**Interactive stereochemistry for undergraduate chemistry.** Turn molecules in 3D, reflect them,
build them, and see how wedge–dash, Fischer, Newman and sawhorse drawings relate to the real
shape. Every practice question comes with an explanation.

Version 1.6.0 · Free edition

## Use it

- **Online:** <https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/chemstereo-lab/>
- **Offline:** download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox,
  Safari), on a computer or a phone. It is one file. Everything it needs is built in, so it works
  with no internet connection, from a laptop or a pen drive.

## What's inside

| Section | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Welcome** | A learning path through the app, with (R)- and (S)-bromochlorofluoromethane turning as mirror images |
| **R/S** | Rank groups by the CIP rules, turn the model so the lowest priority points away, and assign R or S |
| **Mirror lab** | Try to superimpose a molecule on its mirror image; see why one carbon with four different groups is enough |
| **Two centres** | Enantiomers, diastereomers and meso compounds |
| **Drawings** | Convert between wedge–dash, Fischer, Newman and sawhorse projections, with an animation of where the groups go |
| **Optical activity** | Plane-polarised light, the polarimeter, specific rotation and enantiomeric excess |
| **Carbohydrates** | D/L from the Fischer projection, ring formation, α and β anomers, Haworth projections |
| **Amino acids** | L-amino acids, R/S, and why cysteine is the exception |
| **E/Z alkenes** | Assign E or Z with the CIP rules |
| **Conformations** | Newman projections and the energy of rotation about a C–C bond |
| **Rings** | Cyclohexane chairs, ring strain and decalins |
| **Builder** | Build your own molecule (one carbon, two carbons, C=C, or a longer chain) and the app works out its stereochemistry |
| **Concepts** | A compact reference to the definitions and rules used in every section |

Drag a model to turn it. On a keyboard, select the model and use the arrow keys (Shift for bigger
steps). You can download any drawing on the stage as a PNG or SVG picture.

## For teachers

The free edition contains every student section, and you are welcome to use it in class.

The **full edition**, licensed to colleges, departments and coaching centres, adds teacher tools:

- **Question papers** in your college's format: sections and marks, up to four sets, and an answer
  key with a marking scheme and worked solutions
- **Worksheets** with answer keys, to print, save as PDF or download as Word
- **PowerPoint and Moodle export**
- **Class quiz** on the classroom screen, one question at a time
- **Your own questions**, written with structures from the app, by typing or by voice
- **My structures**: save molecules from the Builder and reuse them in questions
- **Projector mode** for large rooms
- **Usage record** for accreditation (ICT-enabled teaching)

When the app is online, it counts visits, the sections opened and quick ratings anonymously, using
GoatCounter. No cookies are used and no personal information is collected. Progress in the R/S
trainer is stored only on your own device.
